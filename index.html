Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

These games are paid apps with no advertising and no in-app purchases. They are designed to work completely offline.

  • No personal data is collected. The games do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • No tracking and no analytics. The games do not use advertising SDKs, analytics services or tracking of any kind, and do not share any data with third parties.
  • Data stored on your device only. Game progress and settings (for example unlocked levels, scores and sound on/off) are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the game.
  • Children. Because no data is collected, the games are safe for players of all ages.

Payments for the games are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment details.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
